## 📄 Article Notes 

### Mathematical Foundations
Asymptotic notations compare growth rates using inequalities and constants entirely for large integer values $n \ge n_0$.

**Why it's crucial:** 
We cannot physically test an algorithm for infinite inputs. Asymptotic math enables us to conclusively prove that eventually, for a large enough input, Algorithm A will *always* beat Algorithm B.

*   By identifying bounds, developers select optimal data structures.
*   It simplifies equations by abstracting away minor operations.
*   It handles both Time Complexity (Time ~ Operations) and Space Complexity (Auxiliary Memory).
